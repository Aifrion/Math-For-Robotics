Math for Robotics

Interactive notes for CSE 276C — Mathematics for Robotics (UCSD, Fall 2026, Prof. Henrik Christensen). Each lecture becomes a page of plain-words notes with live demos you can poke at.

The one-sentence version: robots turn every real problem — reaching, estimating, planning — into numerical math, and this course is the toolbox: linear systems, interpolation, approximation, root finding, optimization, integration, and subspaces.
