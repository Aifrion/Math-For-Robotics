Math for Robotics

Interactive notes for CSE 276C — Mathematics for Robotics (UCSD, Fall 2026, Prof. Henrik Christensen). Each lecture becomes a page of plain-words notes with live demos you can poke at.

The one-sentence version: robots turn every real problem — reaching, estimating, planning — into numerical math, and this course is the toolbox: linear systems, interpolation, approximation, root finding, optimization, integration, and subspaces.

Lectures

Lecture 1 · Transformations

Frames, rotation matrices, Euler angles & gimbal lock, quaternions, homogeneous transforms.

Lecture 2 · Linear Systems

Row view vs column view, null space, Gauss/LDU, eigen-decomposition, QR, SVD — with the 2-link arm.

Lecture 3 · coming soon

Interpolation: linear, Lagrange, splines, bilinear, kriging. (Demos already live in the HW2 page.)

Lecture 4 · coming soon

Function approximation: minimax, Chebyshev, series truncation.

Lecture 5 · Root Finding

Bisection, secant, Brent, Newton-Raphson, Sturm counting, deflation, Müller.

Lecture 6 · Optimization

Golden section, downhill simplex, Powell, conjugate gradient, annealing, DP.

Lecture 7 · Integration & ODEs

Trapezoid to Romberg, RK4, adaptive steps, Bulirsch-Stoer, stiffness, PDEs.

Lecture 8 · Subspace Methods

Eigenimages, PCA and the small-matrix trick, Fisherfaces, CCA, ICA.

Homework companions

HW2 · Trajectory Interpolation

Reconstructing a self-driving-car trajectory by interpolation, and designing a cos-table for a robot with no FPU.
